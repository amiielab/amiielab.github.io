Research at AMIIE

The Applied Machine Intelligence Initiatives & Education (AMIIE) Lab conducts interdisciplinary research in artificial intelligence and machine learning with an emphasis on real-world impact. Our work spans healthcare and biomedical AI, responsible and explainable AI, computer vision and multimodal learning, graph-based machine learning, generative AI, and intelligent decision-support systems.

Across these areas, we are particularly interested in building AI systems that are accurate, interpretable, reliable, fair, and useful in practice. Our research combines methodological development with application-driven studies and collaboration across computing, healthcare, and other scientific disciplines.

Core Research Areas

Five complementary areas shape AMIIE's current research portfolio.

AI for Healthcare & Biomedical Research

AI methods for medical imaging, health data science, biomedical informatics, clinical prediction, and data-driven decision support.

Responsible, Explainable & Fair AI

Research on interpretability, explainability, reliability, fairness, bias, and trustworthy AI in high-impact applications.

Computer Vision & Multimodal AI

Learning systems for images and multimodal data, including representation learning, image understanding, and limited-data learning.

Graph Machine Learning & Biomedical Networks

Graph neural networks and network-based learning for biological relationships, structured knowledge, high-dimensional biomedical data, and discovery.

Generative AI, LLMs & AI Agents

Research on reasoning, reliability, explainability, evaluation, safety, tool use, and responsible agentic AI systems.

Current Research & Selected Work

Current directions and representative recent work that illustrate the lab's research portfolio.

Healthcare AI

AI for Healthcare & Biomedical Research

We develop AI and machine-learning methods for medical imaging, health data science, biomedical informatics, clinical prediction, and decision support, with emphasis on practical and interpretable models.

Selected Work

  • KneeXNet-2.5D: a clinically-oriented and explainable deep learning framework for MRI-based knee cartilage and meniscus segmentation. npj Health Systems, 2026.
  • Predicting cancer survival at different stages: Insights from fair and explainable machine learning approaches. International Journal of Medical Informatics, 2025.
Trustworthy AI

Responsible, Explainable & Fair AI

We study interpretability, explainability, fairness, reliability, and bias in AI systems, particularly in settings where predictions and model behavior must be transparent and trustworthy.

Selected Work

  • State-of-the-art in responsible, explainable, and fair AI for medical image analysis. IEEE Access, 2025.
  • Explainable Contrastive Learning for KL Grading Classification in Knee Osteoarthritis. IEEE EMBC, 2025.
Vision & Multimodal

Computer Vision & Multimodal AI

Our work includes medical image analysis, representation learning, limited-data learning, image understanding, and methods that combine visual information with textual or structured data.

Current Emphasis

  • Explainable and data-efficient medical image analysis.
  • Image segmentation, classification, and representation learning.
  • Multimodal learning across visual, textual, and structured information.
Ongoing Direction

Graph Machine Learning & Biomedical Networks

We are exploring graph neural networks and network-based approaches for biological and biomedical data, where relationships among genes, proteins, clinical variables, patients, or other entities are represented explicitly.

Current Emphasis

  • Graph neural networks for biomedical prediction and discovery.
  • Biological interaction networks and patient-level data integration.
  • Interpretable graph learning for biomarker and knowledge discovery.
Emerging Direction

Generative AI, LLMs & AI Agents

We investigate generative AI and agentic systems with a focus on reasoning, reliability, explainability, evaluation, and responsible use. A current question is whether agent-based workflows improve reliability and safety over conventional LLMs.

Selected Work & Direction

  • Explainable Feature Engineering in Health Data Science: Empirical Comparison of ChatGPT-4o and Classical Machine Learning Methods. ACM/IEEE CHASE, 2025.
  • Ongoing research on AI agents, agentic workflows, reasoning, reliability, and safety.
Scientific Knowledge

Computational Text Analytics & Scientific Knowledge

AMIIE also studies methods for extracting knowledge from scientific, biomedical, and health-related text. Earlier work in datasets, embeddings, and text mining now connects naturally with LLMs, retrieval-based methods, and scientific discovery.

Current Emphasis

  • Biomedical and scientific text mining.
  • Open scientific datasets and terminology discovery.
  • LLMs, retrieval-based systems, and scientific knowledge discovery.

Our Research Approach

AMIIE emphasizes interdisciplinary, student-engaged research that connects methodological AI innovation with meaningful applications. We aim to develop research that is technically strong, interpretable, reproducible, responsible, and capable of contributing to real-world scientific and societal needs.
