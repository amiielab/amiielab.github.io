Student Research Highlight

Soumya at the IBM/IEEE AI Compute Symposium 2026

CareCompute: Compute-Aware Selective Agentic AI for False-Negative Rescue

On October 28, 2026, Soumya Sri Perepu presented her research at the IBM Thomas J. Watson Research Center in Yorktown Heights, New York. Her project investigates selective computation, intelligent routing, and human-in-the-loop oversight in AI-assisted clinical decision-making.

Research advisor: Dr. Soheyla Amirian, AMIIE Lab
