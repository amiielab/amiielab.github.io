About AMIIE

The Applied Machine Intelligence Initiatives & Education (AMIIE) Lab at Pace University is an interdisciplinary research and education environment focused on responsible and explainable AI, healthcare and biomedical AI, computer vision and multimodal learning, graph machine learning, generative AI, and AI agents. We connect methodological innovation with meaningful applications, student mentorship, and interdisciplinary collaboration.

Soheyla Amirian, Ph.D.

Director, AMIIE Lab · Assistant Professor, Pace University

Research Areas

Focused research at the intersection of intelligent systems, health, science, and responsible AI.

Computer Vision & Multimodal AI

Graph Machine Learning
